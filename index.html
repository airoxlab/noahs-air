What we do

Heating and cooling, start to finish.

Repairs when something quits, installs when it is past saving, and the service in between that stops it quitting in the first place.

A/C repair

When it is 105° outside, a dead air conditioner is not an inconvenience. Diagnosed properly and fixed.

Heating & furnaces

High-desert nights get cold. Furnaces that will not light, short-cycle or blow cool air, put right.

Heat pumps

One system for both seasons — efficient in a climate that swings hard in both directions.

Mini-splits

Ductless units for garages, additions, converted rooms and anywhere the ducting never reached.

New system installation

Sized for your home, not guessed at. Old unit pulled out and taken away.

Maintenance

A service before summer is cheaper than an emergency call in the middle of it.
